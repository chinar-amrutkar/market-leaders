Watchlist methodology

RS = Stock daily close ÷ SPY daily close

A stock qualifies when its RS is strictly greater than the highest RS value across the preceding 250 trading sessions. The current session is excluded from that comparison. A ticker appears if it had at least one breakout in the latest 10 completed sessions. This identifies relative-strength leaders to monitor; it is not an entry signal.

Eligibility filters: share price above $10; 30-day average dollar volume above $30 million; 10-day average share volume above 350,000 shares; 14-period ADR% above 4%; biotech stocks included. Dollar volume is calculated as 30-day SMA multiplied by 30-day average share volume.

Data and coverage

TradingView's public screener supplies the NASDAQ, NYSE and AMEX common-stock and depositary-receipt universe. Daily closes for stocks and SPY come from Yahoo Finance via yfinance. Every stock series is aligned to SPY's exact trading dates before calculating relative strength. These are public data interfaces, not an institutional market-data service. Newly listed shares with insufficient history cannot qualify. Use the list to find candidates, then assess pullbacks to support or moving averages, price action, volume and risk/reward on the chart before considering an entry.
